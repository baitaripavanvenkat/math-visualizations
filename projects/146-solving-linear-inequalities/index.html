Rule reminder

When you multiply or divide both sides of an inequality by a negative number, the inequality reverses direction.

× (−1) ⇄
◉

Why it works

1 Linear inequality represents a range

A linear inequality compares a variable expression to a number and describes all values that make the comparison true.

2x + 3 < 11
2 Add and subtract the same amount

Subtract 3 from both sides to isolate the variable term without changing the inequality direction.

2x + 3 − 3 < 11 − 3 ⇒ 2x < 8
3 Divide by the coefficient

Divide both sides by 2 to solve for x. Since 2 is positive, the inequality keeps the same direction.

2x ÷ 2 < 8 ÷ 2 ⇒ x < 4
4 Negative coefficient rule

If you multiply or divide by a negative number, the order reverses: < becomes > and vice versa.

−2x > 6 ⇒ x < −3
