Why it works
1

Locus definition

A circle is the set of all points P such that the distance from P to the center C is r.

2

Equal distance

As P moves, CP stays equal to r. All such points form a circle.

3

Equation obtained

Using the distance formula:
(x - h)² + (y - k)² = r²

Visual Proof

Let P(x, y) be any point on the circle with center C(h, k) and radius r.

Distance CP = √((x - h)² + (y - k)²) = r → (x - h)² + (y - k)² = r²  ✓

Thus, the locus of P is a circle.
