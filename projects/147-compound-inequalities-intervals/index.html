In set notation: { x | -2 < x < 4 } = (-2, 4)
1. AND (Intersection $\cap$): An AND compound inequality $a < x < c$ means $x > a$ AND $x < c$. The solution is the overlap (intersection) of the two regions. If the two rays do not meet, the solution is the empty set $\varnothing$.
2. OR (Union $\cup$): An OR compound inequality $x < a \text{ OR } x > c$ includes any number satisfying either condition. This forms a union of disjoint rays $(-\infty, a) \cup (c, \infty)$, or covers all real numbers $\mathbb{R}$ if the rays overlap.
3. Open Circle $\circ$ vs Parenthesis $($: Strict inequalities ($<$ or $>$) do not include the boundary value. On the number line, this is indicated with a hollow/open circle, and in interval notation with a parenthesis.
4. Closed Circle $\bullet$ vs Bracket $[$: Inclusive inequalities ($\le$ or $\ge$) include the boundary value. This is represented by a solid/closed circle and in interval notation with a square bracket.
